<section class="profile-hero" aria-labelledby="intro-title">
  <h1 id="intro-title">About me</h1>
  <p class="profile-lead">I am a Senior Researcher at Qualcomm AI Research in Amsterdam.</p>
  <p>I completed my PhD at <a href="https://vu.nl/en">Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam</a>, advised by <a href="https://jmtomczak.github.io/">Jakub Tomczak</a> and <a href="https://staff.fnwi.uva.nl/m.welling/">Max Welling</a>. Previously, I worked with Microsoft AI4Science, Qualcomm AI Research, and NVIDIA.</p>
</section>

<section class="profile-focus" aria-labelledby="focus-title">
  <p class="profile-section-label">Research interests</p>
  <h2 id="focus-title">Efficient LLM architectures</h2>
  <p>My current work explores latent reasoning, compressed KV caches, and sub-quadratic attention, with an emphasis on reducing the memory and computational cost of language models.</p>
</section>
